Hi, I'm Anway. I build neural interfaces.

I'm Anway Pimpalkar, a PhD student in Engineering Sciences at Harvard University.

I build robotic interfaces that bridge engineering and biology to restore or augment human function. By integrating physiological principles with mechatronic engineering, I hope my work will enable neuromodulatory approaches for medical and rehabilitation technologies. My work is supported by the NDSEG Fellowship and Harvard SEAS Prize Fellowship.

Before moving to Cambridge, I received my Master's in Biomedical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University and my Bachelor's in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from College of Engineering Pune, India.
